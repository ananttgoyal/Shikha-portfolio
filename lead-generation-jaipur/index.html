See how a lead moves

From “I’m interested” to a clear outcome.

Open a stage to see what your team should know.

  1. 01Enquiry received

    Record the source and the customer’s requirement in one place, so the enquiry is visible to the team.

    Keep visible: source + requirement
  2. 02Owner assigned

    Give one team member responsibility for responding and keeping the record current.

    Keep visible: owner + response due
  3. 03Follow-up planned

    Set the next call, message or meeting with a date. Every open enquiry needs a next action.

    Keep visible: next action + date
  4. 04Sales conversation

    Understand fit, answer questions and record what the customer needs before a decision.

    Keep visible: need + decision blocker
  5. 05Outcome recorded

    Record won, lost or not ready, with the reason. For a sale, agree the handover to delivery or operations.

    Keep visible: outcome + handover
  6. 06Weekly review

    Review pending responses, overdue follow-ups and outcomes. Choose one process improvement with an owner.

    Keep visible: bottleneck + action owner

Common questions

Before we start.

Is advertising spend included?

No. The ₹50,000 starting monthly service fee is separate from advertising spend. We agree the budget and scope before launch.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No fixed number is guaranteed. Results depend on your offer, audience, budget and sales response. We agree useful measures and review progress together.

Can you help if we already receive leads?

Yes. We check response times, ownership and follow-up first. A focused Sprint may fit better than a new advertising campaign.

How soon will work begin producing results?

We first confirm the offer, access and tracking, then set the launch plan. Campaign learning and sales cycles vary; we review weekly rather than promise an instant result.
