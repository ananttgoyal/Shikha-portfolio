Service Delivery Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Shikha Solutions provides business consulting, growth advisory and event services. We do not sell or ship physical products, so no shipping charges apply.

When delivery starts

  • 7-Day Growth Sprint: after payment, we contact you on WhatsApp/email within 1 working day to schedule the Day 0 call. The Sprint starts within 7 days of payment, on a date agreed with you, and runs for 7 days.
  • On-Site Sprint: includes one visit to your business within Jaipur on the agreed date. Visits outside Jaipur are agreed and quoted separately before payment.
  • Monthly packages: work begins on the start date in your proposal; deliverables follow the scope agreed in writing.
  • Events: the seat confirmation is emailed after payment; venue details are shared on WhatsApp before the event.

How services are delivered

Services are delivered through video/phone calls, WhatsApp, email, shared documents and, where agreed, in-person visits. Digital deliverables (plans, trackers, reports) are shared by email or a shared-drive link.

Delays

If we cannot begin within the timelines above, we will tell you and offer a new date or a refund as per our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
