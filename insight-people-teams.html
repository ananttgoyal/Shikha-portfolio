People & Teams · Evidence-led guide 02

Your team may not need more motivation. They may need a clearer finish line.

Accountability becomes actionable when a role has a visible outcome, decision authority and review rhythm.

By Aanant Goyal · Published 1 September 2026 · Last updated 2 September 2026 · 8–10 minute read

Research-informed, practically appliedPublished evidence plus an operating framework for founder-led SMEs. No claim that one method works identically everywhere.

What the research tells us

Role ambiguity is not a soft issue

Tubre and Collins found a negative relationship between role ambiguity and job performance, with variation by job and rating source. A job description does not guarantee performance, but unclear expectations create avoidable friction.

Specific goals outperform vague instructions

A goal-setting meta-analysis found strong support for specific, difficult goals compared with easy or “do your best” goals. It also supported combining goals with feedback.

Skills and management practices interact

The OECD treats managerial and workforce skills as related productivity drivers. Clear goals without capability create pressure; capability without outcomes creates activity.

What this means for a founder-led SME

Replace activity language with an operating contract. “Own collections” becomes “By Thursday, every invoice over 30 days has an owner, next action and expected receipt date; escalate disputes above ₹X.”

A practical 30-day implementation

  1. Step 1Choose one role that requires repeated founder follow-up.
  2. Step 2Define three business outcomes, not every activity.
  3. Step 3Add evidence of completion, decision rights and escalation triggers.
  4. Step 4Run the same 20-minute weekly review for four weeks.
  5. Step 5Diagnose clarity, capability, capacity and commitment separately.

What to measure

  • Outcomes completed by review date
  • Items returned due to unclear completion criteria
  • Founder reminders per week
  • Skill gaps identified and closed
Founder ActionWrite: “By [date], [owner] will deliver [observable output], measured by [evidence], with authority to [boundary].”
Evidence boundary

Specific targets can distort complex work. Use learning goals and coaching where capability is still developing.
