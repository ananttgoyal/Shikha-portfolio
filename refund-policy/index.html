Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy applies to payments made to Shikha Solutions through this website, payment links or Razorpay. If a signed proposal or agreement gives different terms for your engagement, the written agreement applies.

7-Day Growth Sprint (Remote ₹5,000 · On-Site ₹10,000)

  • Before the Day 0 call: cancel any time before the Day 0 kick-off call for a 100% refund.
  • After Day 0 has started: the Sprint fee is non-refundable because the work, time and on-site slot are already committed. You may pause once and resume within 30 days.
  • Rescheduling: the Day 0 call or on-site visit can be rescheduled free once with at least 24 hours’ notice.
  • If we cannot deliver: if Shikha Solutions cancels or cannot start the Sprint within 14 days of payment, you receive a full refund.

Monthly packages and custom engagements

Monthly fees are billed in advance as per your proposal. You can stop at the end of any paid month by giving written notice before the next billing date. Fees for a month already started are not refunded. Any one-time setup fee is non-refundable once work has begun.

Events (e.g. Sur & Samvad)

  • Full refund if you cancel at least 5 days before the event date.
  • Within 5 days of the event, the fee is non-refundable, but you may transfer your seat to another founder (with our approval) or to the next edition.
  • If we cancel or postpone the event, you may choose a full refund or a seat at the new date.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to the original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet). Your bank may take a few additional days to show the credit. Payment gateway charges are not deducted from refunds that are due because of our cancellation.

Failed or duplicate payments

If money is debited but the payment fails, it is automatically reversed by Razorpay/your bank, usually within 5–7 working days. If you are charged twice for the same order, write to us with both payment IDs and the duplicate is refunded in full.

How to request a cancellation or refund

Email anant@shikhasolutions.com with your name, phone number and Razorpay payment ID. We acknowledge requests within 2 working days.
