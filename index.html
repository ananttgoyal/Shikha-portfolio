Frequently asked questions

Questions before we speak

Who does Shikha Solutions work with?

Our primary focus is coaching and training institutes that want better admissions, counselling, marketing and team execution. We also support clinics, startups and growing SMEs with sales, operations and practical business systems.

What happens during a Free Admission Leak Audit?

In a free 30-minute call, we understand your institute's enquiry sources, counselling and follow-up process, then identify one useful starting point. You leave with a practical next step. This is a diagnosis conversation, not a promise of admissions.

Can you help with marketing as well as counsellor training?

Yes. Depending on your priorities, our work can connect Instagram content, website enquiries, WhatsApp follow-up, lead trackers, counsellor training and referral relationships. We agree the scope before starting.

Do you only advise, or also help execute?

We help implement the agreed work alongside your team, including follow-up routines, sales reviews, content, useful trackers and role clarity. Deliverables and responsibilities depend on the engagement.

What does it cost to start?

The Admission Leak Audit is free. A seven-day Admissions Sprint is ₹5,000 remotely or ₹10,000 on-site in Jaipur. Monthly Institute Growth Support starts at ₹50,000 per month, with a minimum three-month engagement. The exact scope and any additional expenses are discussed before work begins.
