Before you sell the course,
understand who opens the door.

An enquiry may come from a student. The decision may involve a parent, a payer or someone they trust. Map the people, their concerns and your next conversation.

One enquiry. Five questions.

  1. Who is learning?
  2. Who is deciding or paying?
  3. Who influences their choice?
  4. What concern remains unanswered?
  5. Who owns the next step?

Use the map with your counsellor

Turn a vague follow-up
into a useful next conversation.

01 · 3 minutes

Choose one real enquiry

Pick a recent enquiry that went quiet. Use an enquiry ID rather than personal contact details on a shared worksheet.

02 · 7 minutes

Map people and concerns

Write what the student wants and what each decision maker needs to understand. Mark unknowns to ask about; do not guess.

03 · 5 minutes

Agree the next action

Choose a helpful next step, one counsellor and a date. Review whether the enquiry moved forward and why.

A simple example

Illustrative, not a client result: A student asks about a course. A parent wants to understand hostel supervision and the full cost. Instead of repeating “Any update?”, the counsellor offers a hostel visit and a written cost breakdown, with the student’s agreement.

For an adult learner, involve parents or other contacts only with their permission. Give clear answers and respect a decision not to proceed.
