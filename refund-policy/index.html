Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy applies to fixed-fee services paid online through this website, currently the Remote 7-Day Growth Sprint (₹5,000) and the On-Site 7-Day Growth Sprint (₹10,000). Engagements agreed through a written proposal follow the cancellation terms in that proposal. Shikha Solutions is operated by Aanant Goyal, Jaipur.

Cancelling before the Sprint starts

The Sprint starts with the Day 0 session, where we agree the metric and baseline. You can cancel at any time before Day 0 takes place and receive a full refund, with no deductions. To cancel, email anant@shikhasolutions.com or WhatsApp +91 77588 59995 with your Payment ID.

After the Sprint has started

Once Day 0 has taken place, time and work have been committed to your business, so the fee is not refundable. If you need to pause, you can reschedule the remaining days once, as long as the Sprint is completed within 30 days of payment.

Rescheduling sessions and on-site visits

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to move the Day 0 session or the Jaipur on-site visit. A session or visit missed without notice may be treated as delivered. The Day 0 session itself can be rescheduled without losing your right to a full refund, provided the Sprint has not yet started.

If we cannot deliver

If we cannot schedule Day 0 within 14 days of your payment, or if we cancel the Sprint for any reason, you will receive a full refund. If, before Day 0, we jointly find that the Sprint is not a good fit for your business, we will refund you in full.

Results

The Sprint is a fixed-scope execution engagement. Business results depend on factors outside our control, so a refund cannot be claimed because a particular sales, admissions or profit result was not achieved. Refunds are governed by the cancellation terms above.

Failed, duplicate or excess payments

If money is deducted but the payment fails, your bank or Razorpay normally reverses it automatically within 5–7 working days. If you are charged twice for the same Sprint, tell us and we will refund the duplicate payment in full.

How and when refunds are paid

Approved refunds are initiated within 2 working days of approval to the original payment method (the same UPI ID, card or bank account) through Razorpay. Banks usually credit the amount within 5–7 working days after it is initiated. We do not charge cancellation fees on refunds made under this policy. We will email you the refund reference.
