Baat karne se pehle

Aapke kuch sawaal.

Kya yeh mere jaise business ke liye hai?

Hum startup founders, growing SMEs, coaching institutes aur healthcare businesses ke saath sales, team aur systems par kaam karte hain. Pehli conversation mein dekhenge ki aapki zaroorat aur hamara support fit hota hai ya nahi.

Pehli conversation mein kya hoga?

Aapki situation aur current process samjhenge, ek priority identify karenge aur practical next step discuss karenge. Coaching institutes ke liye Admission Leak Audit ka option bhi hai. Aage kaam karna compulsory nahi.

Sirf advice milegi ya execution bhi?

Hum agreed scope ke hisaab se aapki team ke saath kaam implement karte hain—jaise follow-up process, trackers, content, CRM ya team reviews. Deliverables aur responsibilities shuru karne se pehle clear hote hain.

Shuru karne ka cost kya hai?

Growth Score aur first discovery call free hain. 7-Day Growth Sprint ₹5,000 remote ya ₹10,000 on-site Jaipur hai. Monthly packages ka scope aur fees services page par dekh sakte hain.

Kya sales ya growth ki guarantee hai?

Nahi. Sales, profit ya admissions ki guarantee nahi dete. Hum agreed work, ownership aur progress ko track karte hain; results aapke offer, market, team aur execution par bhi depend karte hain.
