The Pehla Darwaza Method

Before you sell the course,
understand who opens the door.

The student may enquire, a parent may pay, and a teacher or friend may influence the decision. Pehla Darwaza helps your counsellor identify these people, understand the unanswered concern and agree one useful next step—instead of repeating “Any update?”

Before you book

A few practical answers.

What will we cover in the free audit?

We will use the 30-minute call to discuss your enquiry journey, counselling and follow-up, identify a likely admissions leak and suggest a useful next step. It is an initial diagnostic conversation, not a complete operational audit.

Do we have to complete all four steps?

No. The offers are options, not a compulsory sequence. After the free call, we agree whether you need one focused Sprint, monthly support, expansion planning or a change your team can make internally.

Can you guarantee more admissions?

No. Admissions depend on your offer, demand, delivery and execution. We agree a baseline and the work to be done, then review actual progress.

Do we need a CRM before working together?

No. A useful tracker can be enough to start. We clarify enquiry stages, ownership and follow-up before recommending software.
