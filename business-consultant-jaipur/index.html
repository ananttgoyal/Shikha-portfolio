Questions before we speak

Is this suitable for a small business?

Yes. The starting point is the problem and your available people, time and budget. We agree a scope your business can carry.

Do we need a CRM first?

No. We clarify the process and ownership first, then decide whether a shared tracker or CRM is the right fit.

Will you work directly with us?

Yes. You work with Aanant on the agreed priorities. Any additional team contribution is defined in the scope.

Do you guarantee sales growth?

No. Outcomes depend on your market, offer, resources and execution. We agree useful measures and review progress against the starting position.
