Service Delivery Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Shikha Solutions provides business consulting and execution support services. We do not sell or ship physical goods, so no shipping or courier charges apply. This page explains how and when services paid online are delivered.

How services are delivered

Remote 7-Day Growth Sprint: delivered through video or phone calls, WhatsApp and email, plus any working documents or trackers shared online.
On-Site 7-Day Growth Sprint: the same remote support, plus one in-person visit of up to 4 hours at your business premises within Jaipur city limits. Visits outside Jaipur are possible only if travel costs are agreed in writing beforehand.

Timelines

After successful payment you receive an emailed receipt immediately. We contact you on your registered mobile number or email within 1 working day to schedule Day 0. Day 0 normally takes place within 7 days of payment, at a time that suits you. The Sprint then runs for seven consecutive days from Day 0, unless we agree a different working schedule in writing.

What is delivered

  • Day 0: one agreed growth metric, its baseline and the one lever we will work on
  • Days 1–6: focused execution support on that lever
  • Day 7: measurement against the baseline and a written next-step recommendation

Confirmation and invoices

Your payment receipt is emailed to the address you enter at checkout. A tax invoice, where applicable, is emailed separately. If you entered a GSTIN at checkout, it will be used for your invoice.

Delays

If we cannot schedule Day 0 within 14 days of payment, you can choose a later date or a full refund, as set out in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
