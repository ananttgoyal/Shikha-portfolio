Frequently asked questions

Before you book a conversation.

Who is Shikha Solutions designed for?

Business and startup owners who are growing but experiencing founder dependency, inconsistent execution, unclear ownership, process gaps or limited performance visibility.

What happens during a Growth Discovery Call?

We discuss your operating context, identify the constraint that deserves attention first and outline a practical next step.

Is this only strategy and advice?

No. The approach combines diagnosis and practical design with hands-on execution alongside the founder and team.

How long does an engagement take?

The 7-Day Growth Sprint is a fixed short engagement around one metric. The 90-Day Growth Execution Partnership is a minimum 90-day engagement focused on 2–3 agreed priority areas, with the scope finalised after the Growth Discovery Call.
