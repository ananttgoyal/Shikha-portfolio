Process & Operations · Evidence-led guide 03

An SOP is not the process. The process is what people actually follow.

A useful process makes inputs, outputs, owners, controls and feedback visible.

By Aanant Goyal · Published 1 September 2026 · Last updated 2 September 2026 · 8–10 minute read

Research-informed, practically appliedPublished evidence plus an operating framework for founder-led SMEs. No claim that one method works identically everywhere.

What the research tells us

ISO promotes a process approach, not paperwork

ISO highlights the process approach, risk-based thinking, documented information, monitoring and continual improvement. It describes defining responsibilities, controlling variation and using data.

PDCA is a learning cycle

ISO frames Plan–Do–Check–Act as setting objectives, implementing, comparing results and acting to improve. The value lies in closing the loop.

Formal practices can support SME productivity

The OECD includes logistics, certification and quality control among practices commonly reported as productivity determinants.

What this means for a founder-led SME

Begin at a costly handoff—sales to delivery, purchase to accounts or production to dispatch. Observe real work before writing the standard.

Example: sales-to-delivery handoff

Delivery repeatedly calls sales for scope details after a deal closes. The team observes ten handoffs and finds four required fields are often missing. They create a one-page acceptance checklist, name the delivery coordinator as receiver and start the turnaround clock only after acceptance. First-pass acceptance and rework are tracked for four weeks. The SOP is updated from real exceptions, not from assumptions in a conference room.

Diagnostic questions before you act

  1. Where does work wait between two roles?
  2. Which missing input causes the most rework or customer delay?
  3. Who owns the performance of the whole process, not only one task?

A practical 30-day implementation

  1. Step 1Select one handoff using delay, rework, complaints, cash impact or escalation data.
  2. Step 2Walk one real case with the people doing the work.
  3. Step 3Record inputs, owner, decisions, output and failure modes on one page.
  4. Step 4Test the minimum standard on ten cases; capture exceptions.
  5. Step 5Assign a process owner and review two measures monthly.

What to measure

  • First-pass completion rate
  • Average turnaround time
  • Rework or return rate
  • Founder escalations
Founder ActionMap one handoff: required input, sending owner, receiving owner, turnaround and proof of completion.
Evidence boundary

ISO offers management principles; this is not a claim that certification itself improves every SME.
