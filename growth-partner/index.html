Common questions

Before we work together.

Will Aanant work directly with us?

Yes. The offer includes weekly strategy and review with the founder, alongside hands-on support for agreed priorities. Any additional team contribution is defined in the scope.

Does this include all content, ads and CRM services?

No. We agree the support needed for your priorities. Specific deliverables, team time and third-party costs are confirmed before starting.

What happens in the first month?

We establish the baseline, agree the priority outcomes and owners, and begin the selected execution work. The sequence depends on access, team readiness and the business constraint.

Can you guarantee growth?

No revenue or growth figure is guaranteed. Outcomes depend on the market, business decisions and execution. We agree measures and review progress honestly.

What happens after 90 days?

We review results, remaining constraints and what the team can now own. Any continuation is discussed against the next priorities and agreed separately.
