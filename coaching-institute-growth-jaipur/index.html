Where growth gets stuck

The 3 places admissions leak.

Late callbacks

A 2011 US study found first-hour contact attempts nearly 7× as likely to qualify leads versus attempts an hour later.

Qualification means a meaningful decision-maker conversation—not an admission. This is cross-industry evidence, not an Indian coaching benchmark. HBR research ↗

No second follow-up

One unanswered call is not a completed admissions process. Agree useful next steps—a callback, demo or follow-up—and record who will make each one.

Only the founder can sell

When you explain the course, parents feel confident. When the counsellor explains it, they “think about it.” Give the team the questions, evidence and decision boundaries that make your approach repeatable.

You’re not behind.

Redseer’s October 2026 test-prep analysis reports that 71% of Tier 1 and 67% of Tier 2+ aspirants were unwilling to relocate to a key hub. It puts annual costs at ₹1.1–1.4 lakh in non-hub cities versus ₹2.5–2.8 lakh in hubs—roughly half.

Our takeaway: local demand gives institutes an opportunity. A dependable teaching, admissions and support system helps you earn that trust.

Source: Redseer, 13 October 2026 ↗ · Findings relate to test preparation, not every type of course.

Marketing within the rules

Build confidence with evidence.

The Ministry of Education’s 2024 model guidelines caution against rank and marks guarantees and misleading claims. The CCPA’s 2024 coaching-advertising guidelines prohibit false or misleading claims about outcomes, faculty, fees and refunds.

We focus your marketing on what families can evaluate: your faculty, how you teach, real student journeys with appropriate consent, and clear fees and refund policies. Results need context—not a promise that every learner will achieve the same outcome.

General information, not legal advice. State requirements and applicability should be checked for your institute. Ministry guidelines ↗ · CCPA guidance ↗

The Pehla Darwaza Method

Be known before students start shortlisting.

Before choosing an institute, many students consult someone they trust. That person is the “first door”. We help you identify these relationships, give something useful first and build a referral channel with clear follow-through—rather than competing only at the final ad or walk-in.

Before you book

Answers to common institute growth questions.

Our institute gets enquiries but admissions are not growing. Can you help?

We start by reviewing how quickly enquiries are answered, whether counsellors understand the learner's needs, how parents' concerns are handled and what happens after the first call. The first priority comes from your actual records, not a promised conversion rate.

Do you handle Instagram, websites and WhatsApp along with counselling?

Yes, within an agreed engagement. Content and website enquiries should connect to a responsible counsellor, a useful WhatsApp follow-up and a simple admissions tracker. We agree which channels and processes are in scope before implementation.

What is the difference between the Audit, Sprint and monthly support?

The free Admission Leak Audit is a 30-minute diagnostic call. The seven-day Admissions Sprint addresses one measurable process problem at ₹5,000 remotely or ₹10,000 on-site in Jaipur. Institute Growth System support starts at ₹50,000 per month with a three-month minimum, for several linked priorities. Branch planning is separately scoped.

Do you promise admissions or ranks?

No. We work on improving the admissions process, counselling and execution with agreed priorities and baselines. Student decisions and exam outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Is this for small institutes?

Yes. You can begin with a register or spreadsheet, clear responsibilities and a consistent follow-up routine. An expensive CRM is not a prerequisite.

Which courses do you work with?

Aanant's direct academy experience is in aviation training. The methods can be adapted for test preparation, professional education and skill-development institutes after discussing your learners, course and admissions journey.

Do you only advise, or also execute?

We work alongside the relevant team to implement agreed changes to enquiry tracking, counselling routines, training, referral development and content. The Sprint or monthly scope sets the responsibilities and deliverables.

When should we start?

An off-season can be useful for improving the system before enquiries increase. If admissions are already open, begin with the largest current gap rather than waiting for the next intake.
