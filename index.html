Frequently asked questions

Before you book a conversation.

Who is Shikha Solutions designed for?

Founder-led businesses that are growing but experiencing dependency on the founder, inconsistent execution, unclear ownership, process gaps or limited visibility into performance.

What happens during a Growth Conversation?

We discuss your operating context, identify the constraint that may deserve attention first and outline a practical next step. You can bring your assessment result if you have completed it, but it is not required.

Is this only strategy and advice?

No. The intended approach combines diagnosis and practical design with hands-on execution alongside the founder and team. The exact level of involvement is agreed before an engagement begins.

How long does an engagement take?

There is no standard duration before understanding the problem. A Growth Conversation is used to define a focused scope, realistic working rhythm and measurable outcome.

Do I need to commit before taking the assessment?

No. The Business Health Assessment is a starting point for clarity. You can review your results before deciding whether to message or book a conversation.
