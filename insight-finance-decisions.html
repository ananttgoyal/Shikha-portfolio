Finance & Decision-Making · Evidence-led guide 05

More reports do not create clarity. Better questions do.

A decision dashboard connects a small number of reliable measures to thresholds, owners and actions.

By Aanant Goyal · Published 1 September 2026 · Last updated 2 September 2026 · 8–10 minute read

Research-informed, practically appliedPublished evidence plus an operating framework for founder-led SMEs. No claim that one method works identically everywhere.

What the research tells us

MSME financial capability deserves attention

The OECD’s 2023 work analyses MSME financial knowledge, behaviour, inclusion and digitalisation using owner-manager data. Using financial information is a management capability, not only an accounts task.

ISO names evidence-based decision-making

ISO guidance includes monitoring, measurement and improving systems based on evidence. Evidence does not remove uncertainty; it makes assumptions reviewable.

Accounting is part of SME management practice

The OECD includes accounting among formal practices associated in the literature with productivity. A dashboard should help the business act, not merely report.

What this means for a founder-led SME

Start from decisions: can we hire, which customers need collection action, where is margin leaking, which product needs attention and how much cash is committed?

A practical 30-day implementation

  1. Step 1List five recurring leadership decisions.
  2. Step 2For each, name the measure, source, owner, frequency and threshold.
  3. Step 3Build a one-page weekly view with consistent definitions.
  4. Step 4Record decision, owner and follow-up beside each material variance.
  5. Step 5Reconcile monthly and remove measures that never change action.

What to measure

  • Cash position or runway
  • Overdue receivables by age and action
  • Gross-margin signal by useful unit
  • Forecast variance and data error rate
Founder ActionAsk: “What decision changes if this number moves?” Keep measures with a clear answer and owner.
Evidence boundary

Indicators vary by sector and accounting method. This is an operating framework, not accounting, tax or investment advice.
