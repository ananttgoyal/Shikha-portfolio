Common questions

Before we start.

What happens during the monthly shoot?

We agree the ideas and scripts first, then guide filming at one Jaipur location. Consistent Presence includes up to three hours; Original Ideas & Credibility includes up to five. We confirm preparation and the shot list in advance.

Can I review the content and request changes?

Yes. We agree an approval process and revision scope before starting. Reel duration, carousel length and feedback deadlines are also confirmed, so both sides know what is included.

Which platforms do you manage?

Both options cover Instagram publishing and Facebook cross-posting, with Stories repurposed from the content we create. Dedicated LinkedIn, YouTube or additional platform management can be scoped separately.

Does this include ads or guaranteed enquiries?

Paid advertising is separate, and organic reach, followers and enquiries are not guaranteed. If your priority is campaigns and sales follow-up, explore our lead generation services in Jaipur.
