Frequently asked questions

Before you book a conversation.

Who is Shikha Solutions designed for?

Shikha Solutions is for business owners and startup founders who know what they want to improve but need dependable execution support. You may need help with content, lead generation, sales follow-up, practical systems, team ownership or moving an important growth priority forward.

What happens during the Free Discovery Call?

We discuss what you are trying to achieve, what is currently slowing progress and what kind of support would be most useful. You will leave with a clearer next step—whether that means handling it internally, starting with a 7-Day Sprint, choosing a one-time setup or exploring a monthly package. There is no obligation to continue.

Is this only strategy and advice?

No. We first clarify the problem and the outcome, then help execute the agreed work. Depending on the package, this can include creating content, setting up lead flows, improving sales follow-up, building trackers and processes, training the team or reviewing progress with the founder.

How long does an engagement take?

The 7-Day Growth Sprint runs for seven focused days. Business Systems Setup generally takes 2–4 weeks. Content, Leads & Sales and Growth Partner packages run monthly, with a minimum three-month engagement so there is enough time to implement, review and improve the work. The final scope and timeline are confirmed after the discovery call.
