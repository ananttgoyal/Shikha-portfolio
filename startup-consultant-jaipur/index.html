What this means for your business

Scale needs more than a bigger target.

Here is the execution approach Aanant brings to startup work. These are the operating principles used to guide a new engagement, rather than reconstructed case studies of the milestones above.

Build a sales routine people can repeat

Define the audience, offer, enquiry source and follow-up steps. Give each lead an owner and review activity alongside outcomes, so sales depends less on memory or one individual.

Make responsibilities clear as the team grows

Agree who owns each result, what they can decide and when they need help. Use a regular review to resolve blockers and build capability.

Strengthen delivery before adding complexity

Document recurring tasks, customer handovers and exceptions. Check capacity and service consistency before expanding the work.

Use the numbers to improve the process

Start with a baseline and a small set of useful measures. Review what changed and adjust the process with the people doing the work.
