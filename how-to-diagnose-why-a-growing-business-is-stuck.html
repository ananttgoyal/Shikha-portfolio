Phase 2: Diagnose · Root-Cause Clarity

How to diagnose the real reason a growing business is stuck

Start with the business outcome that is not moving, trace where work or decisions break, and test whether the primary constraint is demand, sales, people, process, finance or founder dependency.

By Aanant Goyal Published 2 September 2026 Updated 2 September 2026

“Growth is stuck” is not a diagnosis. It may describe weak demand, false pipeline, delivery capacity, unclear ownership, cash pressure or slow decisions. Solving the wrong layer adds activity without releasing the constraint.

Shikha’s diagnostic sequence

  1. Define the stalled outcome: name the number, customer promise or operating result.
  2. Locate the break: identify where work waits, returns, leaks or escalates.
  3. Separate cause from symptom: ask why three times and demand evidence.
  4. Choose the narrowest constraint: one that, if improved, would release meaningful capacity.
  5. Run a measured test: do not redesign the whole company first.

Diagnostic questions

  • What changed before the result weakened?
  • Is the gap demand, conversion, capacity, quality, cash or decision speed?
  • Where do teams compensate with follow-ups and workarounds?
  • What evidence would disprove the current explanation?
Founder Action

Take one recurring symptom and create a one-page constraint map: desired outcome → current evidence → point of failure → likely cause → two-week test → measure → owner.

Practical takeaway: the constraint brief

Complete: “We expected ___, observed ___, and found work breaks at ___. We believe the cause is ___. For two weeks, ___ owns a test of ___. We will review ___ on ___.”
