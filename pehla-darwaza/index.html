The Pehla Darwaza Method

Reach the first door.
Before the admission decision.

Before choosing an institute, many students ask someone they trust for advice. That person is the “first door”—pehla darwaza.

Most institutes compete at the last step with ads, banners and walk-ins. This method helps you build relationships earlier, where a useful introduction can put your name into the conversation before a shortlist is made.

Start with one question

“Admission se pehle kis se salah li thi?”

Ask your last 30 admitted students who they turned to before joining. Their answers—not assumptions—show you where to start.

An original Shikha Solutions relationship-building method. Results depend on the relationships, course fit and follow-through.

Why this approach can help

Build a relationship before you need a referral.

Trust can carry over

A thoughtful introduction from someone the student trusts can make your institute easier to consider.

You enter the decision earlier

The student can hear your name before comparing institutes or visiting a centre.

Relationships can keep working

Useful, maintained relationships can create repeat introductions. Track the time and cost instead of assuming every referral is free.

Relationships take time to copy

An ad can be copied quickly. A history of being useful and following through takes time to build.

Trust needs honest information

Share clear course information and genuine student journeys with permission. Build confidence without promising admissions, ranks or results.

From mapping to monthly review

The 7 steps.

  1. 01

    Map it.

    Ask your last 30 admitted students: “Admission se pehle kis se salah li thi?” Look for recurring names and roles.

  2. 02

    Pick 1–2 types.

    Choose the people with regular student contact, strong trust and a realistic way for you to build a relationship.

  3. 03

    Give first, ask later.

    Offer something useful with no referral request attached: career guidance at a school, a course-information sheet for a clinic, or a free test for a tutor’s students.

  4. 04

    Keep in touch.

    Plan one thoughtful touchpoint each month: a greeting, an event invitation or a useful update. Share individual student progress only with their permission.

  5. 05

    Make referrals easy and trackable.

    Use a referral card or a WhatsApp link. Add “Who told you about us?” to the enquiry register and give every new enquiry an owner.

  6. 06

    Review every month.

    Count enquiries and admissions by first door. Compare conversion, relationship effort and cost, then focus on the doors that work.

  7. 07

    Check your Growth Score.

    Use the Free Coaching Growth Score to check whether enquiry handling, counselling and follow-up are ready to turn that trust into a good admissions experience.
