Common questions

Before we start.

Is advertising spend included?

No. The ₹50,000 starting monthly service fee is separate from advertising spend. We agree the budget and scope before launch.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No fixed number is guaranteed. Results depend on your offer, audience, budget and sales response. We agree useful measures and review progress together.

Can you help if we already receive leads?

Yes. We check response times, ownership and follow-up first. A focused Sprint may fit better than a new advertising campaign.

How soon will work begin producing results?

We first confirm the offer, access and tracking, then set the launch plan. Campaign learning and sales cycles vary; we review weekly rather than promise an instant result.
