Common questions

Before we set anything up.

Do we need to buy a new CRM?

Not necessarily. We review your current tools and process first. Improving the way your team uses an existing system may be the right starting point.

Will everything on WhatsApp become automatic?

No. We agree which repetitive steps can be supported by the chosen tools and where a person should take over. Account requirements, supported integrations and any recurring costs are confirmed in the scope.

Can you move our existing data?

We review its format, quality and volume before agreeing any migration. Data cleanup and larger migrations are scoped explicitly so the work and responsibilities are clear.

What do you need from us?

One decision maker, a person who owns the workflow, access to the agreed tools and time with the team to understand and test the process.

What happens after handover?

The one-time engagement includes the agreed setup, training and handover. Any continuing support, new workflows or further integrations are discussed separately.
