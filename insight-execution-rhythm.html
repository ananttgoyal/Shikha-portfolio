Execution · Evidence-led guide 07

The problem is rarely a lack of ideas. It is the gap after the meeting.

Execution improves when priorities become specific commitments and feedback arrives quickly.

By Aanant Goyal · Published 1 September 2026 · Last updated 2 September 2026 · 8–10 minute read

Research-informed, practically appliedPublished evidence plus an operating framework for founder-led SMEs. No claim that one method works identically everywhere.

What the research tells us

Specific goals outperform vague goals

The goal-setting meta-analysis found strong support for specificity and difficulty, plus support for goals with feedback. This is evidence against ending with “we should improve this.”

Complexity changes goal effects

Research warns that task complexity moderates goal effects. For unfamiliar work, a learning goal may be better than an arbitrary output target.

PDCA connects action to learning

ISO’s PDCA guidance requires planning, implementation, checking results and acting to improve. A meeting creates value when an item enters that cycle.

What this means for a founder-led SME

Convert each priority into one owner, one next deliverable and one review date. Keep active priorities few enough that daily operations do not silently defeat them.

A practical 30-day implementation

  1. Step 1Pause priorities with no sponsor or capacity.
  2. Step 2Define the next observable deliverable, not the whole ambition.
  3. Step 3Set one owner and earliest useful review date.
  4. Step 4Compare promise with result and diagnose variance.
  5. Step 5Document learning and release capacity before adding work.

What to measure

  • Commitments delivered by review date
  • Average age of overdue commitments
  • Active priorities per owner
  • Reviews ending with owner, deliverable and date
Founder ActionRead back: owner, next deliverable, review date and evidence of completion.
Evidence boundary

Specific targets can distort behaviour when measures are poor. Use learning goals for uncertain work and watch for gaming.
