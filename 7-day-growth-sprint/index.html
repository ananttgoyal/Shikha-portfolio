How it works

A deliberately narrow process.

01 · Baseline

Pick one metric

02 · Execute

Work one lever

Why it exists

Reduce commitment risk.

Founders often do not want another open-ended advisory engagement before they know how the working relationship feels. The Sprint gives both sides a defined problem, a short window and a measurable result.

What it is not

Not a seven-day transformation promise.

The purpose is to improve one agreed metric or execution lever, learn quickly and make the next decision with better evidence.

Frequently asked questions

Before you start.

What is the difference between the Remote and On-Site Sprint?

The structure is the same: one metric, one lever and seven focused days. The On-Site Sprint adds one in-person visit in Jaipur so I can observe the workflow, team and operating environment first-hand.

Do we work on several problems during the seven days?

No. The Sprint works because the scope stays narrow. We choose one metric and one lever at the start.

What happens if the issue turns out to be bigger than expected?

We still finish the agreed Sprint scope, document what we learned and then decide whether a broader engagement makes sense.

Is the Sprint only for sales?

No. The metric can relate to sales, follow-up, ownership, process, review rhythm or another clearly measurable operating gap.

What happens after the Sprint?

You can stop there, run another focused Sprint, or explore the Monthly Growth Retainer if the business needs ongoing execution support.
